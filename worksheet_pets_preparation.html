Sophie's Blog ✏️   My pets are wonderful!
Hi, I'm Sophie! I have two pets: a cat called Whiskers and a goldfish called Bubbles. Every morning, I feed Whiskers with cat food and give Bubbles some fish food. Whiskers is very lazy1 – she usually sleeps for twelve hours a day! But at night she is very active and runs around the flat. I stroke2 her every day because it helps me feel calm after a long school day. My goldfish lives in a big tank and I must clean it every week. Having a pet teaches you to be responsible3 – you can never forget to feed your animal or give it fresh water. Pets are wonderful because you never feel alone when they are at home with you.
